# BACKROOMS BRAWL: Captain Clark

A 2D hybrid platform-brawler based on the drawings of August Spangler.

- **Story Mode:** LV 4 — Water Rooms, then Level 2 — Yellow Rooms
- **VS Mode:** Fight the Balloon Smiler, Crab Boss, Hazmat Explorer, or LV4 Guy
- **Controls:** Keyboard (A/D, W, J, K, L), touch buttons, or an Xbox 360 controller (left stick/D-pad move, A jump, X punch, Y Hat Throw, B/LB/RB block, Start pause)

Art & Story by August Spangler • Directed by August & Corey Spangler • Built by AI

Play: https://hirammcknoxt.github.io/backrooms-brawl/
